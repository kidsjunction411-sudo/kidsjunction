Privacy policy: KidsJunction Publisher

Last updated: 25 November 2026

KidsJunction Publisher ("the app") is an internal tool of the KidsJunction channel. It uploads KidsJunction's own videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to KidsJunction's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public, and it is used only by the KidsJunction team.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos and manage playlists on the KidsJunction channel. The app does not read viewers' data, comments or watch history.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): permission to publish videos and Reels to the KidsJunction Page and Instagram account, and to read those accounts' basic details (name and id).

What the app stores

Only the sign-in tokens needed to publish, kept on the KidsJunction team's own computer. They are not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising. The app collects no personal information about viewers, and none about children.

Children

KidsJunction makes videos for children. Our YouTube videos are marked "made for kids", so YouTube limits data collection on them as required by law (including COPPA). This app does not collect any data from viewers of any age.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove the app's access at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for Meta in Facebook Settings > Business integrations.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message KidsJunction on the Kids Junction Facebook Page or through the YouTube channel's About page.
