Two Tiffins

🎈 Ages 4-8🎈 Ages 8-12🤝 Friends💛 Kindness & manners

⏱ 4 min readहिन्दी में पढ़ें

The new boy arrived on a Monday, holding his school bag with both hands as if it might run away.

"Everyone, this is Kabir," said the teacher. Thirty pairs of eyes turned to look at him. Kabir looked at his shoes. He was shy, the kind of shy that makes your ears go warm. But if you had known him, you would have seen that inside that quiet boy was a very big heart.

At lunchtime the whole class spilled out under the big neem tree. Lids popped, steel boxes clinked, and the air filled with the smell of parathas and pickles. Kabir opened his tiffin happily. Inside were two soft rotis and a little potato sabzi, still warm from his mother's kitchen.

Rohan leaned over and laughed out loud. "Huh! Just roti and aloo? You call that lunch?"

A few children giggled. Kabir's ears went warm again. "My mum made it for me," he said softly. "I really like it."

Meera put her own box down with a bang. "Rohan, you shouldn't laugh at someone's food. Look, you've made him feel bad."

Rohan shrugged, but his smile wobbled. "I was only... joking."

"Kabir, come and sit with us," said Meera, and she moved over to make space. Kabir sat down. "Thank you, Meera," he whispered, and for the first time that day, he smiled.

The next day, something unexpected happened.

Rohan dug through his bag once. Then twice. Then he turned it upside down. Books, pencils and one lonely sock fell out, but no tiffin. "Oh no," he groaned. "I left it at home!" His stomach gave a loud, angry growl, and this time nobody laughed, because they could all see how hungry he was.

Kabir looked at his two rotis. He thought about yesterday. Then he held out his box.

"Rohan, eat with me. There's enough for both of us."

Rohan stared. "You'd share with me? But yesterday I laughed at you."

Kabir just shrugged. "That's okay. Everyone gets hungry."

Rohan took one bite, and his eyes went wide. "Wow! This sabzi is really tasty!" He ate another bite, and then he put the roti down. "Kabir, I'm sorry. I shouldn't have laughed at your lunch. That was mean."

"It's okay," said Kabir, grinning. "Come on. Now we're friends."

"Then tomorrow I'm bringing mine too," said Rohan, "and we'll share two tiffins together!"

And that's exactly what they did, the next day and the day after that and the day after that. Under the big neem tree, two tiffins became one lunch, and two boys became best friends.

⭐ The lesson

Don't judge anyone by what they eat or wear. Share with kindness, and you'll find true friends.

💭 Think about it

If you had been Meera that first day, what would you have said or done when Rohan laughed?
